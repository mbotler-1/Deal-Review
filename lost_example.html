Why This Deal Was Lost

Recorded loss reason: "Budget cut." An inbound demo request created early momentum, but the champion never secured executive budget sign-off. Facing a mid-year spend freeze, the deal slowly stalled — five close-date pushes over three months in Proposal — while the scope was quietly halved ($140K → $85K) without a reset. It ended as a no-decision, not a competitive loss, which makes it re-engageable next budget cycle.

Deal Context

Mid-Market / Direct, new business. Product: Streaming TV. Champion Alex Doyle (Marketing Manager) was enthusiastic but not the economic buyer; the VP of Finance controlled the budget and never joined a call. Process Gaps

Gap 1: No Re-Engage Task at Close

A 'Budget cut' loss is highly re-engageable, yet the opp closed with no re-engage task, future date, or next step logged.

Recommendation: Make a dated re-engage task mandatory in the Closed Lost flow when the reason is budget/timing.
Gap 2: No Executive Sponsor

The deal ran for five months single-threaded on a champion who never had budget authority; the economic buyer never joined a call.

Recommendation: Require an identified economic buyer to advance past Discovery; block Proposal without a mapped budget owner.
Gap 3: Serial Close-Date Slippage

The close date moved five times over three months, each push keeping the deal 'alive' without a changed commitment.

Recommendation: Require a reason on every close-date change; auto-flag any opp with 3+ pushes for a qualify-or-kill review.
Gap 4: Happy-Ears Forecast

Probability sat at 60% in Proposal for ~3 months, then dropped to 0% the day it was lost — the forecast never reflected the deal's health.

Recommendation: Tie probability to objective stage-exit criteria; down-rank stale high-probability opps in the weekly review.
Gap 5: Scope Cut Without Re-Baseline

The amount was halved ($140K → $85K) mid-deal with no reset of plan, timeline, or decision criteria.

Recommendation: Treat a >25% amount change as a re-baseline trigger — reconfirm scope, timeline, and close plan.
Gap 6: Risk Not Escalated

The champion flagged a spend freeze on the April call, but no manager escalation or save-play was logged before the deal died.

Recommendation: Log deal risks as tracked items; trigger a manager review when a budget-freeze risk is raised on an active deal.
